## Fact notes

- No statistics. "An hour of chaos" is a figure of speech, not a measurement. The huddle format
  (what's out, who's where, one focus, a thank you) is common restaurant practice.
- "86'd" is standard US kitchen slang for "out of" or "off the menu", said as "eighty-sixed".

## Post caption

Five minutes before you open can save you an hour of chaos. Keep the huddle to three things: what's 86'd today, who's on which station, and the one thing you're nailing this shift. Same time every shift, standing up, everyone talks once, and end on a thank you.
