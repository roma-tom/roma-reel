## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): 90 seconds a drink at a messy station, 75 seconds
  once everything is within reach, 40 drinks in the rush.
- **Worked calculation:** 90 − 75 = 15 seconds a drink; 15 s × 40 drinks = 600 s = 10 minutes
  of work saved in the rush, with the same team.
- The fix is common bar workflow practice (one-step reach, pre-stacked cups, splitting milk
  and shots between two people), not a statistic.

## Post caption
