# priya-twenty-minutes

**Topic:** Priya waited 20 minutes for one latte
**Kind:** story (see `style/KINDS.md`), **shape B**: an unhappy guest, in her own words, won back
**Hook:** custom (`hook.js`): Priya taps her foot at the pickup counter while the clock spins and
cup after cup slides past her to other guests who pop up and take them
**Background:** sky (pad: drift)
**Voice:** speech; Priya says the hook and her quote, Roma Tom narrates the rest
**Length:** about 38 s

The story: on a Saturday rush, Priya's oat latte ticket slips under the pile and she waits twenty
minutes while twelve drinks go out ahead of her. Roma Tom walks it over himself, apologizes with
no excuses, and puts her next one on the house. She comes back on Monday and nearly every weekday
after. The lesson falls out of the story: the slow drink is a slip; ignoring the guest is the
real mistake.
