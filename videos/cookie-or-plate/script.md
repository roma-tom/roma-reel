## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): a $2 cookie costing 30¢ to make, a $16 plate with
  $5.60 of food (35% food cost), 80 cookies and 12 plates sold a day.
- **Worked calculations:** $2.00 − $0.30 = $1.70; $16.00 − $5.60 = $10.40;
  80 × $1.70 = $136; 12 × $10.40 = $124.80 ("about a hundred and twenty-five", $125 on screen).
- "Doesn't need a cook": cookies are baked in batches ahead; a plate is cooked to order. A
  general point, not a measured labor cost, so it isn't counted in the numbers.

## Post caption

The $2 cookie or the $16 plate: which earns you more? Per sale, the plate wins easily: $10.40 kept against $1.70. But sell 80 cookies and 12 plates a day, and the cookie brings in $136 to the plate's $125. Small margin, big volume. What did you guess?
