## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): a $4 croissant; butter and flour $1.00, the baker's time
  $1.20, bag $0.10, card fee $0.12 (3% of $4), oven gas $0.08. Kept: 4.00 − 2.50 = $1.50.
- **"Say your power bill is $900 a month":** an example inside published ranges for small US
  restaurants and cafes: EIA-based estimates of about $2.90 per square foot a year put a 3,000
  sq ft restaurant near $770 a month (https://electricityplans.com/average-electricity-cost-small-business/ ),
  and Toast cites about $1,290 a month for restaurants
  (https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/average-restaurant-electricity-bill ). A small cafe
  can be well below or above; the line says "say".
- **Worked calculation:** $900 ÷ $1.50 = 600 croissants a month; 600 ÷ 30 days = 20 a day.
