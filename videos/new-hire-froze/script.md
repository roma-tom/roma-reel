## Post caption

Day two. First rush. Theo froze. Nobody had shown him the milk station. His first week should have gone: day one, shadow someone; day two, one station only; day three, a slow afternoon on his own; day five, his first rush, with backup. Never put a new hire on a rush alone, teach one station at a time, and give them one buddy all week.
