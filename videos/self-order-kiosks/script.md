## Post caption

The self-order kiosk: friend or foe? Honestly, both. It's great for the rush and for big orders, but for a regular who wants a hello, a screen is a step backward. If you add one, keep a person at the counter too, keep the screens to one upsell, not five, and greet everyone who walks in.
