## Fact notes

- **Example figures** (said as "Say…"): $50 of food a week thrown out from the back of the
  walk-in. **Worked calculation:** $50 × 52 = $2,600 a year.
- **"Seven days in the fridge, counting the day it's made":** FDA Food Code section 3-501.17:
  refrigerated, ready-to-eat, time/temperature-controlled food prepared on site and held more
  than 24 hours must be date marked and used within 7 days at 41°F or below, with the day of
  preparation counted as day 1. The Food Code is adopted state by state, so the line says
  "most health codes". State summaries: https://www.health.mn.gov/communities/environment/food/docs/fs/datemarkingfs.pdf ,
  https://www.oregon.gov/oha/PH/HEALTHYENVIRONMENTS/FOODSAFETY/Documents/FactSheet27Datemarking.pdf
- FIFO (new stock behind old, labels, a daily check) is standard stock rotation practice, not a
  statistic.
