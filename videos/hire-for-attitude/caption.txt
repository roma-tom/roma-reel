Lots of experience and a good hire sound like the same thing. Often they're not. The register and steaming milk can be taught on the job. Being warm, reliable and easy to coach is much harder to teach. Leadership IQ followed twenty thousand new hires, and nearly nine in ten of the ones who failed did so over attitude, not skill. Decide which skills you truly need on day one, ask how they'd handle a rush, and try a short, paid trial shift.
