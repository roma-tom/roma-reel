One dollar more an hour, or two thousand dollars to replace her?

A barista leaving costs you a job ad, interviews, a week of training pay, your best barista's time as trainer, remade drinks and a slower line. Call it $2,000.

A $1 raise on a thirty-hour week is $1,560 a year. If it keeps her, it pays for itself.

Start with your best people, give the raise before they ask, and tell them why: you want them to stay.
