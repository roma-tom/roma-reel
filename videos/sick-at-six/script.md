## Fact notes

- No statistics. A story with the cast (Ana, Dev, Leo, Roma Tom) and common scheduling
  practice. Some states and cities have paid sick leave and scheduling laws; the video gives no
  legal advice and "never make the sick person find their own cover" is a kindness, not a
  legal claim.

## Post caption

6:02 am, Ana's sick. Who covers? This is where a plan beats a panic. Keep an on-call list for every shift, agree what a short-staffed day looks like, like a smaller menu, and never make the sick person find their own cover.
