## Fact notes

- No statistics. "Eighty percent as good" is a figure of speech (the familiar "80% as good"
  delegation idea), not a measurement. The list and the fix are common delegation advice.

## Post caption

Doing every job yourself? Drop one, on purpose. Hand off first what someone else can do almost as well: opening and closing, ordering stock, and the schedule. Eighty percent as good, by someone else, beats a hundred percent by an owner who's burnt out. Write it down once, show them twice, and check in after a week.
